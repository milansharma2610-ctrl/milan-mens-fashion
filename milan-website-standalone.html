About Milanworld Calzaturificio

Milanworld is a celebration of Italy's grand cordwainer heritage. Born in the artisanal shoemaking heart of Montegranaro and styled on Milan's prestigious Via Montenapoleone, we craft sculptural footwear for discerning connoisseurs. Every pair is cut, lasted, and stitched by master artisans with over 40 years of dedicated craft.
